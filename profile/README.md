
# IAAUTECO-inc

> **Expertise in Open Systems, Digital Sovereignty, and Inclusive AI.**

IAAUTECO is a systems architecture consultancy and applied research hub specializing in the design of resilient, high-performance infrastructures. We bridge over two decades of Linux ecosystem expertise with the strategic imperatives of European technological sovereignty and social innovation.

---

## 🏛️ Institutional Vision & Mission

Our mission is to redefine the relationship between humans and technology through systems that are transparent, auditable, and ethically grounded.

* **Digital Sovereignty:** Securing critical infrastructures and deploying Sovereign AI frameworks.
* **Social Innovation:** Advancing inclusive robotics and applied AI to enhance autonomy for individuals with disabilities.

### ⚖️ Governance & Compliance
We adhere to the most rigorous standards to ensure the integrity of the software supply chain:
* **OpenChain Project (ISO/IEC 5230):** Strict Open Source License Compliance.
* **Security Assurance (ISO/IEC 18974):** Robust security protocols for critical software environments.



# IAAUTECO-inc

> **Architecting Digital Sovereignty and Social Autonomy through Open Systems.**

IAAUTECO is a research and engineering organization dedicated to the design, security, and deployment of open-source infrastructures. Founded on the principles of technological resilience and public interest, we bridge the gap between high-performance systems architecture and societal impact.

---

## 🏛️ Institutional Mission

Our work is structured around two strategic pillars designed to ensure a secure and inclusive technological future for the European ecosystem:

### 1. Digital Sovereignty & Cybersecurity
We develop and secure critical infrastructures built on the **Linux ecosystem**. Our focus lies in creating resilient architectures and security frameworks that align with European institutional standards, ensuring data governance and the mitigation of systemic dependencies.

### 2. Social AI & Inclusive Robotics
We lead open-source research at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence and Robotics**. Our mission is to enhance mobility and autonomy for individuals with disabilities through ethical, accountable, and interoperable systems that prioritize human dignity and digital inclusion.

---

## ⚖️ Standards & Compliance: OpenChain

IAAUTECO is committed to the highest levels of professional open-source management. We align our development workflows with the **OpenChain Project** standards:

* **ISO/IEC 5230:** Maintaining rigorous Open Source License Compliance to ensure trust and transparency across the software supply chain.
* **ISO/IEC 18974:** Implementing robust Open Source Security Assurance for critical software environments.

Our adherence to these protocols ensures that our contributions are audit-ready and suitable for deployment in high-stakes, sovereign environments.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Foundation

With a self-directed technical foundation established in 1999 and over two decades of expertise in **Open Source Systems Architecture**, our technical stack is built for long-term performance:

* **System Engineering:** Low-level development and optimization in `C/C++`.
* **Applied AI & Logic:** Robust implementations using `Python` and `Java`.
* **Data Governance:** Structured and secure data management via `SQL`.
* **Environment:** Advanced Linux hardening, kernel-level security, and sovereign cloud infrastructures.

---

## 🌐 Philosophy: The Open Paradigm

We advocate for a future where **Open Systems** are the default for critical infrastructure. By eliminating "black-box" proprietary constraints, we empower institutions and individuals to regain control over their digital destiny. 

1.  **Accountability:** All algorithms and systems must be auditable.
2.  **Interoperability:** Promoting open standards to prevent vendor lock-in.
3.  **Resilience:** Building for decades, not for update cycles.
